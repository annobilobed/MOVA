## Scope

MOVA public landing page (`/`), Persuade. App screens (trips, seats, checkout, tickets, driver, operator, admin) are Operate and inherit this world through type, palette, density and one signature move. This replaces the earlier "category standard" world (user-requested redesign, 18 Nov 2026).

## Audience and job

UCC students on phones, often on 3G, deciding to book their trip home or a campus shuttle. Action: search trips (origin, destination, date) in working form in the first viewport. Proof: real live trips from the API (demo-labelled), the seat → pay → QR → board mechanism, Paystack mobile money/card. No invented fares, testimonials, GPS or SMS promises.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The night ride home. MOVA is the overnight coach from campus, so the site is the highway at dusk turning into dawn. It refuses the bright SaaS gradient hero with a product card.

OWN-WORLD: Sky gradient as the palette: night indigo #0B1026, midnight #151B3D, plum #5B2E6B, ember #F0803C, dawn gold #FFC46B, morning #A9D8FF. Headlight amber #FFB547 marks every action and selection, with ink text on it. Signal green #3DDC97 means valid/confirmed and tail-light red #FF5A67 means error/held. Overpass (highway-sign lettering) for display, Inter for body, tabular numerals. Dashed lane markings and glowing road studs serve as dividers and progress. Dark = night, light = morning haze.

STORY: A student feels the trip home, searches at once, sees seat → pay → QR → board, sees real departures, and books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed 3D highway at night. A coach drives toward the camera, its headlights lighting the road; lane dashes and studs stream past, street lights and palms sweep by, stars over a sky that cycles dusk→night→dawn about every 60 s. Left: headline "Your journey. Your seat. Your freedom.", one line, then the search as a solid dashboard panel with an amber "Find available trips". Scroll pulls the camera from chase view to roadside view and speeds the bus; the pointer shifts the view.

FORM: Assigned candidate 6 of my ordered list (night bus journey); seed:842e8458c3ab7583. Raises kept from declined challengers: one hue owns action (headlight amber only, from the single-hue program); one unbroken line with a travelling light for progress (from the step row); light appears only where energy flows (from the neon circuit); layouts snap to whole units (from azulejo).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
