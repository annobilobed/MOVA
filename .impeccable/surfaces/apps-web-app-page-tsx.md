## Scope

MOVA public landing page (`/`). Visitor mode: Persuade. App screens (trips, seats, checkout, tickets, operator, admin, driver) are Operate and inherit this world through type, palette, density and one signature move.

## Audience and job

UCC students (mostly on phones, often 3G) deciding whether to book their next trip home or campus shuttle with MOVA. Action: search trips (origin, destination, date) in working form in the first viewport. Proof: real live trips from the API (seed data labelled demo), the seat-pick → pay → QR mechanism shown, Paystack mobile money/card. Constraints: no invented fares, schedules, testimonials, GPS or SMS promises.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard for mobility, played straight at the craft of Uber/Bolt, FlixBus/Omio and Revolut/Monzo: a search-first hero with the product at work, not a mood.

OWN-WORLD: Lavender Mist ground, Ink text; an Electric→Violet field owns the hero; Aqua marks routes and available seats; Coral/Sun only as small highlights. Plus Jakarta Sans display, Inter body with tabular numerals. 12–20px card radii, soft offset shadows, solid forms, glass only on floating hero chips. Dark theme mirrors it on Ink-navy.

STORY: A student sees what MOVA is in one line, searches immediately, understands seat → pay → QR → board, sees real upcoming trips, and books.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top nav (wordmark, Find a Trip, How it works, Help, Sign in). Left: headline "Your journey. Your seat. Your freedom.", one sentence, then the working search panel (From, To, Date, Find available trips) at full width on mobile. Right: stylised 3D coach on a floating aqua route ribbon with two location pins and a floating seat/ticket chip; static fallback. Search never covered.

FORM: canon (standing exit, chosen by the user); seed:f7a1897301419a25. Signature move: the route ribbon — an aqua line that links hero, trip cards, seat hold and ticket.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
